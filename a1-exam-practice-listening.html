Time and scoring

The approximately 20 minutes include the listening procedure and, in the paper exam, about five minutes to transfer answers to the answer sheet. This is not extra time. Each correct answer earns one raw point: 15 raw points in total, weighted to 25 points of the 100-point exam. There is no separate official Hören pass mark.

A simple preparation path

  1. Understand: learn the task types and playback rules below.
  2. Work through: read our original examples and understand why an answer is correct.
  3. Listen: use the official audio practice, first for learning, then under exam conditions.
  4. Review: record the missed detail and repeat targeted practice.

What is available here?

Teil 1 · Short conversations

6 questions · a, b or c · listen twice

Our strategy

  1. Before listening, read the question and all three options. Identify what you need: a time, price, place or other detail.
  2. On the first listening, follow the situation and choose a likely answer. A number can be mentioned without being the answer.
  3. On the second listening, check the exact detail. Pay attention to corrections such as „nein“, „doch“ and „erst“.
  4. Mark one answer only. Do not choose an option just because you heard the same word.

Original format walkthrough · text only

Understand the task before practising with audio

Read the original script, choose an answer, then check the explanation. This checks understanding of the task; it does not measure your listening ability.

Read the sample script

A: Entschuldigung, beginnt der Deutschkurs heute um neun Uhr?
B: Nein, heute beginnen wir erst um halb zehn. Die Lehrerin kommt etwas später.
A: Also um neun Uhr dreißig?
B: Ja, genau.

Wann beginnt der Deutschkurs?

b — 09:30 Uhr. The first time is corrected. „Halb zehn“ means 09:30, and the final reply confirms it.

Next training step

Train times in two forms: „neun Uhr dreißig“ and „halb zehn“. Later, use real audio without looking at the transcript.

Teil 2 · Public announcements

4 questions · true or false · listen once

Our strategy

  1. Read the statement before the announcement. Find the one fact you must check.
  2. Listen to the whole announcement once. Keep the statement in mind instead of trying to translate every word.
  3. Compare the meaning: who, where, when and what has changed? Watch words such as „heute“, „morgen“ and „nicht“.
  4. Choose „Richtig“ or „Falsch“ and move on. In the exam this recording is not repeated.

Original format walkthrough · text only

Understand the task before practising with audio

Read the original script, choose an answer, then check the explanation. This checks understanding of the task; it does not measure your listening ability.

Read the sample script

Liebe Kundinnen und Kunden, unsere Bäckerei schließt heute schon um siebzehn Uhr. Morgen sind wir wieder ab sieben Uhr für Sie da. Vielen Dank.

Die Bäckerei ist heute bis 18 Uhr geöffnet.

Falsch. Today the bakery closes at 17:00, not 18:00. The time 07:00 refers to tomorrow’s opening.

Next training step

Practise separating today’s change from tomorrow’s normal schedule. Use one playback when you move on to audio practice.

Teil 3 · Telephone messages

5 questions · a, b or c · listen twice

Our strategy

  1. Read the question and options first. Decide whether you need a place, reason, time or action.
  2. Use the first listening to understand why the person is calling and what they want.
  3. Use the second listening to check the final arrangement. The first plan may be cancelled or changed.
  4. Choose one option based on the whole message. Listen carefully to „nicht“, „bitte“ and „komm“.

Original format walkthrough · text only

Understand the task before practising with audio

Read the original script, choose an answer, then check the explanation. This checks understanding of the task; it does not measure your listening ability.

Read the sample script

Hallo Mia, hier ist Leon. Heute treffen wir uns nicht im Park. Es regnet. Komm bitte um drei Uhr zu mir nach Hause. Meine Adresse ist Blumenstraße acht. Bis später!

Wo treffen sich Mia und Leon?

c — Bei Leon zu Hause. The park is explicitly cancelled. Leon asks Mia to come to his home. The time and address are extra details for this question.

Next training step

After listening, summarise the final arrangement in one short sentence: who meets whom, where and when?

Train with a clear routine

This is our suggested learning routine, not an official Goethe timetable. Repeat a stage whenever you need more practice.

1

Build your listening basics

Practise numbers, prices, clock times, weekdays, dates and spelling with audio. Contrast pairs such as dreizehn / dreißig and vierzehn / vierzig.

2

Train one task type

Start with one part. Before playback, underline the target detail. Use the real replay rule: twice for Teil 1, once for Teil 2, twice for Teil 3.

3

Review what you missed

After answering, check the solution and transcript. Write down the decisive German phrase and why your answer differed. Replay that short section for learning.

4

Try a full listening section

Use a complete official practice set: 15 questions, no pausing or extra replays, and follow its instructions. In paper practice, transfer answers within the allotted time.

Common traps to recognise

  • Corrections: the first time or place may change later in the recording.
  • Negation: „nicht im Park“ rules out the park, even though you heard „Park“.
  • Time references: keep „heute“ and „morgen“ separate.
  • Distractors: familiar words can occur in incorrect options. Match the meaning of the whole message.

One step at a time.

Start with the format, then build your skills.

Four skills. One A1 exam.

The exam checks how you understand and use German in familiar everyday situations. Each skill has an equal share of the total result. Select a skill to see its parts.

~20 min

Hören

Listening

3 parts · 15 questions

Understand conversations, announcements and messages.

25 min

Reading

3 parts · 15 questions

Find important information in short everyday texts.

20 min

Schreiben

Writing

2 parts · form + message

Complete a form and write a short, useful message.

~15 min

Sprechen

Speaking

3 parts · group exam

Introduce yourself, ask questions and make requests.

Hören~20 minLesen + Schreiben45 minSprechen~15 min

The written exam has no scheduled break. Reading and writing share 45 minutes; 25 minutes for reading and 20 for writing is the suggested split. The speaking appointment is arranged by your examination centre.

Prepare for Sprechen

The oral exam is a group exam with up to four participants. Around 15 minutes is the total group duration, not 15 minutes per person. Assessment concerns task fulfilment and comprehensibility; the section contributes up to 25 exam points.

How to use this page

Learn the format, try the original examples, review the explanations, then use the linked official practice. These learning examples are not a complete mock exam.

Teil 1 · Introduction

Introduce yourself using prompts such as name, age, country, home, languages, occupation and hobbies. Be ready to spell a name and give requested numerical information.

  1. Prepare short true sentences, not a long memorised speech.
  2. Practise spelling your name and saying phone numbers and your age clearly.
  3. Listen to the examiner and give the information requested.

Original practice example

Sich vorstellen

Show model answer and explanation

Ich heiße Mira Shah. Ich bin 26 Jahre alt. Ich komme aus Indien und wohne in Freiburg. Ich spreche Hindi, Englisch und ein bisschen Deutsch. Ich bin Studentin. Meine Hobbys sind Lesen und Wandern.

Use your own details. Practise saying the letters of your surname in German.

Teil 2 · Questions

Use a topic and word card to ask another participant for information. Also answer the questions others ask you.

  1. Connect the word to the topic and form a complete question.
  2. Choose a simple W-question or yes/no question.
  3. Give a short meaningful answer; avoid replying with an unrelated memorised sentence.

Original practice example

Thema: Freizeit

Karte: Wochenende

Say your own question aloud before revealing an example. Then practise both roles with a partner.

Show model answer and explanation

Frage: Was machen Sie am Wochenende?
Antwort: Ich gehe am Samstag spazieren.

The question uses the card and stays within the topic of free time. Other clear, appropriate questions are also possible.

Original practice example

Thema: Wohnen

Karte: Balkon
Show model answer and explanation

Frage: Hat Ihre Wohnung einen Balkon?
Antwort: Ja, meine Wohnung hat einen kleinen Balkon.

Teil 3 · Requests

Use a picture card to make a suitable request and respond to another participant’s request. A question for information and a request for an action are different tasks.

  1. Identify the object or action shown on the card.
  2. Use a polite request such as Können Sie bitte …?
  3. Respond naturally: agree or politely explain why you cannot help.

Original practice example

Bildkarten-Training: Fenster

Ein offenes Fenster

Imagine a picture of an open window. Make a request to close it. This text prompt replaces a picture for learning.

Show model answer and explanation

Bitte: Können Sie bitte das Fenster schließen?
Antwort: Ja, gern.

Original practice example

Bildkarten-Training: Kugelschreiber

Ein Kugelschreiber
Show model answer and explanation

Bitte: Können Sie mir bitte einen Kugelschreiber geben?
Antwort: Ja, hier, bitte.
Alternative: Tut mir leid, ich habe keinen.

Practise aloud

These are text prompts, not speech recognition or an official speaking assessment. Record yourself using your device if useful, or practise with a partner. Ask for feedback on task completion and intelligibility.

Your study plan

A focused practice routine

Suggested study routine, not an official exam timetable. Repeat each stage as needed.

  1. Practise your introduction, alphabet and numbers aloud every day.
  2. Make questions from everyday themes such as home, shopping, travel and free time.
  3. Practise requests and responses in pairs; swap roles and cards.
  4. Watch the official speaking example, then rehearse all three parts with other learners.

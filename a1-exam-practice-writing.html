Teil 1 · Form

Complete five missing details in a form using the supplied situation. Copy facts accurately; the information may concern another person.

  1. Read who the form is for before entering any personal information.
  2. Match each field to its source: family name, city, date, number or payment method.
  3. Check spelling and numbers. Do not invent missing details.

Original practice example

Anmeldung zum Sprachkurs

Ana Costa kommt aus Portugal und wohnt in Bonn. Sie ist 28 Jahre alt. Sie möchte am 8. März mit dem Kurs beginnen. Ihre Telefonnummer ist 0228 765432.

Vorname: Ana (Beispiel)

Show model answer and explanation

Familienname: Costa
Land: Portugal
Wohnort: Bonn
Alter: 28
Kursbeginn: 8. März

Compare all five fields with the situation. The phone number is extra information, not a requested field.

Teil 2 · Message

Write a short message of approximately 30 words addressing three content points. Include an appropriate greeting and closing.

  1. Identify the recipient and choose a suitable greeting and formal or informal address.
  2. Write one or two simple sentences for each content point. Answer all three.
  3. Check verb position, names, dates and punctuation. Aim for clarity rather than complex grammar.

Original practice example

Eine Nachricht an die Sprachschule

Sie möchten einen Deutschkurs besuchen. Schreiben Sie an die Sprachschule:

  • Warum schreiben Sie?
  • Fragen Sie nach einem Abendkurs.
  • Fragen Sie nach dem Preis.

Practise here before opening the model. Your text is not automatically graded or saved.

Show model answer and explanation

Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren,
ich möchte im März Deutsch lernen. Haben Sie einen Abendkurs für Anfänger? Wie viel kostet der Kurs? Bitte schicken Sie mir Informationen.
Mit freundlichen Grüßen
Ana Costa

The message gives a reason, asks about an evening course and asks the price. The greeting and closing suit a school. This is one possible answer, not a compulsory template.

What examiners assess

Teil 1 has five raw points. Teil 2 has up to three raw points for each of the three content points plus one for communicative text design: ten raw points. The 15 raw points are weighted to 25 exam points. A word count alone cannot assess your writing.

Your study plan

A focused practice routine

Suggested study routine, not an official exam timetable. Repeat each stage as needed.

  1. Learn common form fields and practise copying dates, names and addresses accurately.
  2. Practise invitations, apologies, enquiries and appointment changes; cover three points each time.
  3. Write an original message before checking a model; ask a teacher or proficient speaker for feedback.
  4. Try both parts together in 20 minutes without a dictionary or translation tool.

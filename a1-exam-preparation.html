The written exam has no scheduled break. Reading and writing share 45 minutes; 25 minutes for reading and 20 for writing is the suggested split. The speaking appointment is arranged by your examination centre.

How to prepare for listening

Practise times, prices, dates and numbers. Read the question before the recording begins. Listen for changes or corrections rather than selecting the first familiar word you hear.

Your short-message checklist

  • Have I covered all three content points?
  • Is my message understandable?
  • Have I included a suitable greeting and closing?
  • Have I followed the approximately 30-word guidance?
How writing is assessed

The form has 5 raw points. The short message has 10: up to 3 for each content point and 1 for the communicative form of the text. Different understandable answers can be valid; you do not need to memorise one model response.

SPEAKING · APPROX. 15 MINUTES

Sprechen: make a simple conversation.

Normally, up to four candidates take part together. There is no preparation time. Each of the three parts takes approximately five minutes for the group.

PartYour taskAnswer format
Teil 1Introduce yourself

Give basic personal information, then spell a word and say a number.

Introduction + follow-ups
Teil 2Ask and answer questions

Use topic and word cards to exchange everyday information with another candidate.

Questions + answers
Teil 3Make and respond to requests

Use picture or action cards to ask for something and respond appropriately.

Requests + responses

Prepare an introduction covering your name, age, country, place of residence, languages, occupation and hobbies. Also practise spelling and saying phone numbers or other numbers.

How to prepare for speaking

Practise with a partner: take turns asking questions, answering and making requests. Naming a picture alone does not fulfil a request task. Learn flexible sentence patterns so you can respond to a new card.

How your result is calculated

60/100

Overall points needed to pass

Complete all four sections.

You need at least 60 points overall. There is no separate 60% pass requirement for each skill.

Hören25%
Lesen25%
Schreiben25%
Sprechen25%

The written sections together account for 75 points; speaking accounts for 25. Writing and speaking are assessed using criteria, not by matching one fixed answer.

Overall scoreResult
90–100Very good
80–89Good
70–79Satisfactory
60–69Pass
0–59Not passed
Raw points and official conversion

Each skill has a maximum of 15 raw points. Writing is 5 + 10; speaking is 3 + 6 + 6. For the overall score, the section points are multiplied by 1.66, added and rounded to a whole point. The 25% figures above describe the equal weighting.

A few things to know before exam day.

Who is this exam for?

Start Deutsch 1 is the adult A1 exam. Goethe recommends it from age 16; that is a recommendation, not a compulsory minimum age. Fit in Deutsch 1 is a different exam for younger learners.

Are dictionaries, phones or notes allowed?

Dictionaries, mobile phones and your own study notes are not permitted during the exam. Follow the examination centre’s instructions for storing personal items.

Is there a break or speaking preparation time?

The 65-minute written exam has no scheduled break. There is no preparation time for speaking. The speaking appointment may be separate; check your examination centre’s schedule.

Do I need to transfer answers?

In the paper exam, answers must be entered on the answer sheet. Allow time within the exam to transfer them. Write the final short message on the answer sheet. In a digital exam, follow the platform instructions.

Can I retake just one section?

Start Deutsch 1 can only be retaken as a whole. Its four skills are not separately retakable modules.

What should I check before exam day?

Check your booking confirmation for the location, arrival time, identification requirements and speaking appointment. Ask the centre in advance about access arrangements if you need them. Fees, dates and local arrangements depend on the centre.

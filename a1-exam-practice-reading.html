How to use this page

Learn the format, try the original examples, review the explanations, then use the linked official practice. These learning examples are not a complete mock exam.

Teil 1 · Messages

Read short personal messages and decide whether each statement matches the text. There are five questions across two texts in the model format.

  1. Read the statement first: identify who, when, where or what it asks about.
  2. Find evidence in the text; compare the whole meaning, not just a repeated word.
  3. Watch for changes, negation and time expressions such as erst, schon and nicht.

Original practice example

Eine Nachricht von Mila

Hallo Ben, unser Treffen ist am Samstag um 11 Uhr, nicht am Freitag. Wir treffen uns vor der Bibliothek. Bring bitte dein Deutschbuch mit. Liebe Grüße, Mila

Das Treffen ist am Freitag.

The meeting is on Saturday. “Nicht am Freitag” explicitly rules out Friday.

Teil 2 · Information

For each of five situations, choose which of two advertisements or information sources fits the need.

  1. Underline every condition: destination, time, price, activity or age group.
  2. Read both options. Reject a tempting match if one necessary condition fails.
  3. Check direction and opening times carefully before choosing a or b.

Original practice example

Ein Abendkurs

Sie arbeiten bis 17 Uhr und suchen einen Deutschkurs am Abend.

a · Sprachstudio Nord
Deutsch A1: Montag und Mittwoch, 18–19.30 Uhr.

b · Sprachstudio Süd
Deutsch A1: Montag bis Freitag, 9–11 Uhr.

Welcher Kurs passt?

Option a starts after work. Both offer German, but option b is in the morning.

Teil 3 · Notices

Read notices, signs and short public information. Decide whether each of five statements is true or false in that situation.

  1. Identify the place and the rule: who may do what, and when?
  2. Pay attention to nur, außer, geschlossen, ab and bis.
  3. Use the information given; do not answer from your everyday assumptions.

Original practice example

Im Schwimmbad

Heute: Schwimmkurse von 14 bis 16 Uhr. Freies Schwimmen erst ab 16 Uhr.

Um 15 Uhr können Sie ohne Kurs frei schwimmen.

Free swimming starts at 16:00. At 15:00 the pool is being used for courses.

Your study plan

A focused practice routine

Suggested study routine, not an official exam timetable. Repeat each stage as needed.

  1. Build vocabulary for dates, opening hours, transport and everyday messages.
  2. Practise one task type and write down the exact phrase supporting each answer.
  3. Complete all three parts of an official set in 25 minutes, including answer transfer.
  4. Review errors by category: vocabulary, negation, time or a missed condition.
